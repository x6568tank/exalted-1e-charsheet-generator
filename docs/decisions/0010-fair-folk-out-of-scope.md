# 0010 — The Fair Folk are permanently out of scope

**Status:** Accepted, 2026-07-29.

## Problem

Six non-Exalt splats are still unimplemented — Mortals, Heroic Mortals, Godblooded,
Ghosts, Dragon-Kings, Mountain Folk. The Fair Folk are the obvious seventh item on that
list, and their chargen is unlike anything else in 1e.

## Alternatives

* **Implement them last.** Keeps the set complete and commits to an entirely separate
  character model for a splat the maintainer does not run.
* **Leave them unlisted** and let the omission speak. Ambiguous — every reader assumes
  "not yet".

## Decision

**The Fair Folk are never being implemented**, and this is recorded so nobody treats the
gap as a roadmap item. In the maintainer's words: "the Fae can go fuck themselves."

## Consequences

* The non-Exalt list is **six splats, not seven**.
* Do not author their data, request their pages, or add them to a splat selector.
* They remain legitimate *subject matter* inside other splats' rules and stay there —
  Thaumaturgy's Warding and Exorcism aspects list them as targets, and the p.115 rule
  bars them from holding Arts.
