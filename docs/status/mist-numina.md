# The Mist aspect / numina of the mist — READ 2026-08-14, blocked on MODELLING only

The last unauthored piece of the Outcaste book. It was recorded as *page-blocked* since
2026-07-29; it is not, and never needed a sync — the whole mechanic is in
`images/_extracted/The Outcaste.md`, chapter three (pp.109-110, the p.118 Cult sidebar,
and the statted numen Samathi Anja on pp.126-127).

## ⚠ There is no numen effect LIST, and there never was

The open question ("does p.118 carry the numen effect list?") is answered: **no, by
design.** Outcaste p.110, verbatim:

> "The Storyteller works with the player to define one numen effect for each point of
> Essence the character possesses. These should have efficacy similar to that of a Fair
> Folk power or a Terrestrial or Celestial Circle spell. The player chooses a Virtue.
> Spending one point of that Virtue activates an effect. Powerful effects, comparable to
> Celestial Circle sorcery, require two points instead."

**Nothing to author.** Samathi Anja's three effects (Conjure, Shame, Mist — 1-2 Conviction
each, pp.126-127) are one NPC's worked example, not a catalogue. This is the same shape as
Combos and the Crimson Pentacle protocols: player-defined content, not printed content.

## ⚠ Two things CLAUDE.md said about this were WRONG

Both came from paraphrasing the Forest Witch summary table rather than the chapter:

| CLAUDE.md said | The chapter says |
|---|---|
| "an aspect with NO Aspect Abilities" | p.110: she **retains** the physical markings **and Caste/Aspect Abilities**. What she loses is her ELEMENT for magical purposes, her anima abilities, and her favoured Charms |
| "a numen must buy Cult • or Cult ••" | p.110: if she does not have Cult, she **gains** a dot. p.118: each numen **has** • or •• |

## What IS printed

**Data, and small** — the Cult sidebar (p.118): Cult • regains 1 temporary Willpower at
sunrise OR sunset; Cult •• regains 1 temporary Willpower every morning OR evening, plus 2
motes per hour. Cult also reads as her effective Resources rating in Yu-Shan.

**A transformation package** (pp.109-110), which is engine work, not data:

* learns **Dematerialize**, costing **half** her motes to use;
* reforms in one lunar month in the heart of the woods if killed without a
  spirit-destroying Charm;
* hears prayers on a successful prayer roll (core pp.335-336) and may grant a blessing —
  the blessed character rerolls one roll during the activity and takes the better result;
* gains a Cult dot if she has none;
* **no element** for magical purposes: loses anima abilities, cannot manifest her anima,
  has no favoured Charms, and pays the off-element surcharge on ALL Charms — while still
  retaining her physical markings and Caste/Aspect Abilities;
* changes shape, and may alter shape freely among suitable shapes for 1 mote;
* gains up to one **blight** (normally Multiple Limbs), one **affliction** and three
  **poxes**, using the Wyld pox rules (core pp.280-281). **If she gained a blight, an
  affliction or both, she loses one dot of Temperance.** Blights inflict no Derangements;
* requires a **sacrifice** beforehand or faces a convalescence potentially exceeding a
  century — examples: enough unworked orichalcum/moonsilver/starmetal/soulsteel to build
  a statue of her; a level 3 jade artifact; three level 3-4 jade artifacts; the
  still-beating heart of a Celestial Exalt; a level 4-5 Hearthstone. Not required if she
  steps into a slain numen's role — but then she becomes THAT entity rather than refining
  herself as she wishes;
* not normally open to mortals or Celestial Exalts (a Celestial who went through it would
  have her Exaltation shard flee to Yu-Shan, considering her dead).

## DEFERRED INDEFINITELY — human's call, 2026-08-14

> *"This feels very similar to Exalted God-Blooded in that it's a very specific
> sub-section of a splat that can be indefinitely deferred."*

Treat this as a **no** unless the human reopens it. Do not propose it, plan around it, or
offer it as a follow-up — the same standing the training-times hedge has.

**The reason it defers cleanly, which is worth understanding before anyone reopens it:**
the build would add almost nothing. The numen effects — the actual substance of being a
numen — are ST-and-player-defined by the book's own instruction, so there is no catalogue
to hold. What is left is a transformation package that reshapes one Dragon-Blood's Charm
economy, for a mechanic whose entire printed population is about eight NPCs. That is
engine work whose output is a sheet restating what the Storyteller already decided.

⚠ **This is NOT a gap.** Any future gap sweep that lists the Mist aspect as unauthored is
counting a deferral as an oversight — the same trap `catalogue-sweep.md` records as
*"missing from the build is not should be authored."*

## If it is ever reopened: how to model it

It is not an aspect in the sense the other five are — it is a **post-Exaltation
transformation** that strips a Dragon-Blood's element and rewires her Charm economy.
Open questions for the human:

1. An `origin` on the Dragon-Blooded splat, a caste/aspect row, or a FLAG that applies a
   package (the Death's Taint shape)?
2. Do the ST-defined numen effects get a free-text rated list on the sheet — N slots where
   N = permanent Essence, each carrying a Virtue and a 1-or-2-point cost — or nothing?
